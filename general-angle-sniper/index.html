🧭 How to Play

  • • 0° is the right-pointing horizontal axis (Positive X).
  • • Positive angles rotate counter-clockwise.
  • • Negative angles rotate clockwise.
  • • One full circle is 360°. Angles larger than 360° keep wrapping around!
  • • Scoring: You start each round with 1000 points. The timer drains them over 20 seconds.
  • • Accuracy matters:
    🎯 Bullseye (within 5°): Keep 100% of remaining points.
    👌 Close (within 15°): Keep 50% of remaining points.
    ❌ Miss (off by >15°): Lose 200 points from total score.
