Year 9 Extension - Week 3

Trigonometry, bearings, and algebra review.

1.
A tree is 7 m tall and casts a 24 m shadow. Find the angle of elevation \(\theta\).
Correct Answer: 16.26
\(\theta=\tan^{-1}(7/24)\approx16.26^\circ\).
2.
A plane heads on \(051^\circ\)T. What is the return bearing?
Correct Answer: B
Add 180 degrees.
3.
Factorise \(3x^2 - 48\) completely.
Correct Answer: 3(x-4)(x+4)
Take out 3, then difference of squares.
4.
Solve \(5(2x + 5) = 2x + 13\).
Correct Answer: -3/2
\(10x+25=2x+13\Rightarrow8x=-12\Rightarrow x=-\frac32\).
5.
Challenge: Solve simultaneously \(y=x^2-2x-3\) and \(y=2x+2\). Enter both points.
Correct Answer: (-1,0),(5,12)
Set equations equal, solve quadratic, then substitute.
