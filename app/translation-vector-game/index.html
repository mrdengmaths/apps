How to Play

Your goal is to find the translation vector that moves the Original (A) shape to the Image (A') shape.

A vector is written as:

(
x y
)
  • Top number (x) is the horizontal shift.
    • Positive (+) = Move to the Right
    • Negative (-) = Move to the Left
  • Bottom number (y) is the vertical shift.
    • Positive (+) = Move Up
    • Negative (-) = Move Down

Count the grid squares to find the horizontal (x) and vertical (y) shifts.

You will be given a translation vector.

The Original (A) and Image (A') shapes will start in the same place.

Your goal is to drag the purple (A') shape to its correct new position, based on the vector given. The shape will snap to the grid.

Let go of the mouse to check your answer!
