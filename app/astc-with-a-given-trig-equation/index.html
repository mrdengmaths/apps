How to Play

You will be presented with 1 or 2 trigonometric conditions (e.g., sin(θ) > 0).

Your goal is to click the correct Quadrant on the circular number plane that satisfies ALL given conditions.

If multiple quadrants are correct, you must click ALL correct quadrants to win the round!

Answer quickly to earn more points. You have 6 rounds. Good luck!
