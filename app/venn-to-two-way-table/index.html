How to Play

Your goal is to fill the two-way table using the numbers from the Venn diagram.

  • The overlapping region of the circles (A and B) goes into the cell for 'A and B'.
  • The part of circle A that doesn't overlap is 'A only'.
  • The part of circle B that doesn't overlap is 'B only'.
  • The number outside the circles is 'Neither A nor B'.
  • 'Total' cells are the sum of the corresponding row or column.

Help & Formulas

Remember these key relationships:

Total A = (A only) + (A and B)

Total B = (B only) + (A and B)

Grand Total = Sum of all 4 Venn regions
