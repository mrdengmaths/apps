📜 How to Play 📜

Welcome, Ninja! Your mission is to simplify the giant fraction before time runs out.

  • To simplify, divide both the top and bottom numbers by their Greatest Common Factor.
  • Type your smaller, simplified fraction into the boxes and hit Submit.
  • If the fraction can't be divided any further, quickly click Already Simplified!
  • Speed matters! You start with 1000 points per round. The longer you take, the lower your points drop.
  • Mistakes cost 100 points, but you can keep trying until you get it right!
