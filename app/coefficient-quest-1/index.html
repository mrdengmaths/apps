What is a Coefficient?

A coefficient is the number part of a term that includes a variable.

  • In 3x, the coefficient is 3.
  • In -5y2, the coefficient is -5.
  • In z, the coefficient is 1 (it's invisible!).
  • In -a, the coefficient is -1.
  • For a constant term like 15, the term itself is the coefficient, so it's 15.
