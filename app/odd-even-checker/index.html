How to Play

Your goal is to determine the symmetry of the graphed function.

Even Functions

Mirror perfectly left-to-right across the vertical Y-axis.
f(x) = f(-x)

Odd Functions

Have 180° point symmetry around the origin. If you flip them left-to-right AND upside-down, they match the original shape.
-f(x) = f(-x)

Neither

Functions that don't fit into either of those symmetry categories.

💡 Tip: In Practice Mode, use the Flip buttons to test the symmetry yourself!
