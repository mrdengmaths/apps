How to Construct

SSS (Side-Side-Side)

Drag the ends of the 3 lines. They will snap together magnetically. Connect all 3 to form a closed triangle.

SAS (Side-Angle-Side)

Place the 30° Angle. Snap the 4u and 8u sides to the vertex. Drag the angle tips to rotate it.

ASA (Angle-Side-Angle)

Attach both angles (45° and 60°) to the ENDS of the 10u side. The intersection determines the 3rd vertex.

AAS (Angle-Angle-Side)

Attach one angle to the side. Place the other angle floating in space (representing the opposite angle). Align arms to form the triangle.

RHS (Right-Hypotenuse-Side)

Place the 90° Angle. Snap the 6u Side to the vertex. Snap the 10u Hypotenuse to the end of the 6u side.
