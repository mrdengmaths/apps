How to Find the Equation

Your goal is to find the equation y = mx + b.

1. Find the Gradient (m)

The gradient (or slope) is the "steepness" of the line. Pick any two points from the table, let's say (x₁, y₁) and (x₂, y₂).

m = (change in y) / (change in x) = (y₂ - y₁) / (x₂ - x₁)

2. Find the Y-intercept (b)

The y-intercept is the point where the line crosses the y-axis. This happens when x = 0.

  • If the table includes x=0: The value of 'y' at that point is your 'b'. Simple!
  • If the table does not include x=0:
    1. Calculate 'm' first.
    2. Pick any (x, y) pair from the table.
    3. Substitute 'm', 'x', and 'y' into the equation: y = mx + b.
    4. Solve for 'b'.
