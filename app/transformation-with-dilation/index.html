Symmetry & Transformations

Visualize how mathematics shifts shapes through reflections and dilations:

  • Drag the curve to shift (translate) it across the plane.
  • Click the curve to track a coordinate point through animations.
  • Negative Dilations: Using a negative factor will first run a 3D Reflection, then scale the graph to demonstrate full horizontal/vertical sign mapping.
  • Intercept Tracking: Click near an axis or use the buttons to accurately mark intercepts. They will dynamically slide along the curve to stay glued to the axis!

X-Axis Reflection

Flips the graph vertically (up/down). The y-values change sign.
f(x) → -f(x)

Y-Axis Reflection

Flips the graph horizontally (left/right). The x-values change sign.
f(x) → f(-x)

Line y = x Reflection

Swaps the x and y coordinates, reflecting across the diagonal. This creates the Inverse Function.
f(x) → f⁻¹(x)
