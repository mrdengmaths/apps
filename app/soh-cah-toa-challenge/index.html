Which trigonometric ratio connects the two highlighted sides for angle θ?

Cheat Sheet

Remember the magic word SOH CAH TOA to find the right ratio. First, find angle θ, then identify the sides:

SOH

Sine = Opposite / Hypotenuse

CAH

Cosine = Adjacent / Hypotenuse

TOA

Tangent = Opposite / Adjacent

  • Hypotenuse: Always the longest side, directly across from the 90° right angle.
  • Opposite: The side directly across from the marked angle θ.
  • Adjacent: The side next to (touching) the marked angle θ, which is not the hypotenuse.
