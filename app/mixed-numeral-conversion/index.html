How to Transform 🧙‍♂️

Mixed to Improper

Use the M.A.D. method!

  1. Multiply the whole number by the bottom (denominator).
  2. Add the top number (numerator). That's your new top number!
  3. Denominator stays the exact same!
Example: 3 ½ ➔ (3×2)+1 = 7 ➔ 7/2

Improper to Mixed

  1. Divide the top by the bottom! The answer is your large whole number.
  2. The remainder (what's left over) is your new top number.
  3. The bottom stays the same!
Example: 11/4 ➔ 11÷4 is 2 Remainder 3 ➔ 2 ¾
