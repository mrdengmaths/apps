How to Play

To identify the correct side, always look at the highlighted line relative to the marked angle θ (theta) and the right angle (square).

Hypotenuse

The longest side of the triangle. It is always directly opposite the right angle (90°) square.

Opposite

The side directly across the triangle from the marked angle θ. It does not touch angle θ at all.

Adjacent

The side that is "next to" or touching the marked angle θ (but is NOT the longest hypotenuse side).
