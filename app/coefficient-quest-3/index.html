What is a Coefficient?

The coefficient is the number part that is multiplied by a variable.

  • In the term 3x, the coefficient is 3.
  • In the term -5y²z, the coefficient is -5.
  • If a variable stands alone, like z, the coefficient is 1.
  • If it's negative, like -ab³, the coefficient is -1.
  • A constant term (a number by itself), like 12, is its own coefficient. The coefficient is 12.
