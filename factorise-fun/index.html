How to Use

  1. Choose Your Challenge: Use the switches to select between 2 or 3 terms, include higher powers, or include negative factors.
  2. Factorise: Look at the expression and type your factorised answer in the box. Use the `^` symbol for powers (e.g., `2x^2`).
  3. Check Your Work: Hit the 'Check' button or press Enter.
  4. Next Question: If you are correct, the 'Check' button becomes a 'Next' button. Click it or press Enter to continue.
  5. Learn and Improve: Read the specific feedback carefully! It will help you find your mistake. If you're still stuck, click the 'Help' button.
